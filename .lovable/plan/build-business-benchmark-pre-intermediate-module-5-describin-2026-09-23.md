# Build Business Benchmark Pre-Intermediate — Module 5: Describing Equipment

Build out the five existing Module 5 skill tiles using the uploaded Student’s Book, Teacher’s Book, Study Book, photocopiable activity, and Track 6 recording. Match the interaction style of completed Modules 1–4 while updating dated workplace references for today’s students.

## Content approach

Preserve the book’s useful language, exam-style reading skills, listening task, and answer keys, but add clear modern equivalents rather than presenting 2006 equipment as current:

- Replace or pair outdated everyday examples such as CD-ROMs, fax machines and standalone memory sticks with USB-C hubs, wireless chargers, webcams, headsets, portable SSDs, smart displays and video-conferencing equipment.
- Keep specialist tools in the “gizmo game” because describing an unfamiliar object is the point of the listening task.
- Frame leasing in today’s context: rental and subscription models, hybrid-work equipment, upgrades, maintenance, secure data removal, energy efficiency, repair and recycling.
- Update equipment problems to include connectivity, pairing, charging, software updates, overheating and battery health, while retaining `keep + -ing` and `need + -ing`.
- Use modern support-ticket and email layouts instead of reproducing dated inbox graphics.

## What gets built

The five greyed-out tiles become clickable activity pages:

1. **Reading**
   - “Leasing equipment” as a polished business web article.
   - Multiple-choice vocabulary and sentence-order/cohesion tasks from the Student’s Book.
   - Fastequip section-heading and right/wrong/doesn’t-say activities from the Teacher’s Book.
   - “Office repairs” email scanning task that completes a repair request form.
   - Short “Then and now” note and discussion on subscription equipment, sustainability and data security.

2. **Listening**
   - Track 6 player with the full transcript in a collapsible section.
   - Pre-listening synonym and temporary/permanent checks.
   - Three-speaker gist and detail activities for the gizmo descriptions.
   - “Who is telling the truth?” answer check with an explanation.
   - Track 6 is the only audio referenced in the uploaded Module 5 materials, so no additional recording is required.

3. **Writing**
   - Interactive repair/support request form based on the two emails.
   - Guided complaint email about the faulty leased shredder, with the required content points, word count and model answer.
   - A modern support-ticket version covering device, location/user, fault, troubleshooting attempted, urgency and requested action.

4. **Speaking**
   - Structured language support for describing parts, shape, material and purpose when the name is unknown.
   - Guess-the-object prompts using a mix of traditional office tools and current workplace technology.
   - Pair-work gizmo challenge and a short workplace troubleshooting role-play.

5. **Vocabulary**
   - Component-parts matching, materials and shape noun/adjective practice.
   - Office-object description matching.
   - Raw materials and product matching from the Study Book.
   - Electrical-equipment phrasal verbs.
   - `keep + -ing` and `need + -ing` repair-language transformations.
   - Modern equipment vocabulary extension with visual prompts.

All objective exercises will provide instant answer checking, useful hints, and right/wrong results recorded with the existing activity tracking.
